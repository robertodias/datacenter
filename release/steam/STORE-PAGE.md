# Hyperscale: Datacenter Tycoon — draft store copy

## Short description
Build a datacenter empire in a town with opinions. Buy land, serve eccentric AI
companies, fund research and keep the servers running through heatwaves, outages
and citizen protests. Grow your campus without losing your neighbors.

## About this game
Your first campus starts with an old warehouse, a substation and a cooling plant.
Your next customer thinks it needs just one more GPU. Your neighbors disagree.

**Build the infrastructure behind the AI boom.** Buy and clear parcels, convert
space into server halls, and grow into GPU clusters and neural superclusters.
Balance power, cooling, local utility reach and the cost of keeping everything online.

**Meet nine fictional customers with very real capacity demands.** Train FreeAGI's
next assistant, host Synthopic's very careful help desk, coordinate Amazing's
warehouse robots and keep Megahard's meetings about meetings running. Projects pay
on delivery; recurring services reward reliability. Review the terms before signing.

**Decide who gets the next compute-hour.** Research competes directly with paying
customers. Develop engineering, commercial or experimental advantages. Hire
technicians, engineers and commercial teams, then manage their costs and priorities.

**Keep the lights on.** Heatwaves, grid interruptions and equipment wear put your
promises to the test. Batteries, generators, maintenance and temporary cooling each
solve a different problem. Prepared operators can protect both revenue and trust.

**Grow with the town.** Citizens respond to noise, traffic, displacement, water use,
jobs and the services your campus provides. Listen to petitions, negotiate commitments,
preserve landmarks or pay for real housing and public projects. Ignoring the cause
can lead to demonstrations and restrictions on expansion.

**Three towns, three dilemmas.** Learn the trade in Littlebrook, manage heat and
water constraints in Sunsprawl, and preserve Oldbridge's heritage while expanding.
Continue after completing a charter, or build freely with a sandbox starting budget.

The standalone demo includes local saves, a skippable introduction, adjustable text,
remappable core controls, reduced motion, original isometric art and adaptive music.
