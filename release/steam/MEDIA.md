# Release media

## Screenshots
Seven actual 1920×1080 renders are in `screenshots`. Suggested first five: growing
campus, first campus, Sunsprawl, Oldbridge, and company contracts. Research and
fictional company profiles provide two additional management views. These are
unmodified game renders; financial state comes from normal paid simulation actions.
The captured session uses isolated QA saves and is staged for demonstration.

## Gameplay footage
`Hyperscale-gameplay.mp4`: approximately 51 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264,
YUV 4:2:0 limited-range BT.709, about 8 Mbps video, stereo AAC at 48 kHz.
The score/ambience are the game's original audio; the export is loudness-normalized.
It shows the first campus, contract board, advanced campus, research and two further
towns. No unreleased feature is mocked up in the footage. It is a staged montage,
not a benchmark or an uninterrupted campaign playthrough.

Recreate with `tests/capture_release.gd` and Godot Movie Maker, then encode the AVI
with FFmpeg. Use `--qa --film`, a 1920×1080 viewport and a fixed 30 fps capture clock.
The generation script is excluded from the shipped game pack.

## Capsule drafts
`capsules/header.png` 920×430; `small.png` 462×174; `main.png` 1232×706;
`vertical.png` 748×896. Original layouts use the project's town sprites and title.
They contain no review scores, sales claims, awards or promotional slogans.
The layouts are generated by `tests/render_capsules.gd` and `tests/capsule_canvas.gd`.
Review actual Steam crops and any additional library assets after creating the app.
These drafts have not been uploaded or approved by Steam.

Dimensions and media preparation follow the [official store graphics guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/standard)
and [trailer guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer). This media kit
is preparation for publication, not evidence that store review has been completed.
